📱 CheckMED
Medication reminders · alarms · schedules · intake log

CheckMED is a medication management app that keeps reminders, schedules, and intake logs simple.
It is designed to help users manage recurring medication routines and review plans and progress at a glance.
Medication-related information is stored on your device (local storage), not on our servers.

※ CheckMED is a medication management aid. It is not a medical device, medical service, or emergency service.

👤 Who it’s for

  • 🕒 Caregivers who want simple reminders for parents or family members who may forget doses
  • 👵 Anyone who needs an easy reminder app for less tech-savvy family members
  • 💊 People managing long-term medication schedules
  • 📅 Anyone who wants alarms and intake logs in one lightweight place
  • 🔐 Users who care about privacy for health-related data
  • ⚡ Anyone who prefers a fast, simple app over heavy “health suite” tools

Whether you support a family member’s schedule or need reminders yourself,
CheckMED can be a straightforward medication routine helper.

💊 Focused on reminders

CheckMED focuses on reminders, alarms, and intake logs.

  • Enter clinic/pharmacy names, treatment period, and time windows → full schedule generated
  • Recurring plans managed automatically
  • Mark taken / missed and keep a history
  • Per-plan progress (completion rate, remaining doses)
  • Long-term schedule management

If you are looking for a medication alarm, schedule helper, or intake log, try CheckMED.

🔔 Reliable notification design

CheckMED is designed with notification reliability in mind.

  • Alarms aligned to the times you set
  • Reminders are scheduled to work even when the app is not actively open, depending on device settings and operating system behavior
  • Time-based scheduling is used to help reduce delays and missed alerts
  • Simple create/edit flows and a clear UI

※ Actual behavior may vary due to device settings, notification permissions, battery optimization, focus modes, manufacturer policies, reboots, and other operating system conditions.

🧭 Quick start

  1. Create a plan: tap “+” → enter clinic/pharmacy/period → set times → save
  2. Get reminders: receive alarms at the configured times
  3. Confirm intake: log as taken or missed
    • Within 30 minutes: waiting (green)
    • Within 1 hour: waiting (orange)
    • After 1 hour: elapsed → marked as missed
  4. Track progress: completion rate / remaining doses in the plan list
  5. Check volume: Check your device’s notification/media volume

⚠️ Notes

  • Long plans (stable reminders):
    On some devices, reminders scheduled far in advance may require the app to be opened periodically to remain active. For best reliability, we recommend opening the app occasionally—especially before long intervals pass or after system, battery, or time-zone changes.
  • Long plans (processing time): editing plans, resetting logs, or deleting a plan may take longer when there is a large amount of data.
  • On some devices, battery optimization or notification settings may change how reminders behave.

🔐 Privacy-first design

Many medication apps rely on server storage. CheckMED defaults to keeping your data on the device.

  • Plan data is stored in local storage and is not uploaded by us to our servers
  • Sensitive data is encrypted with AES-256; keys are protected via Android Keystore / iOS Keychain
  • No account required
  • Requests only minimal permissions needed for reminder functionality
  • If advertising Software Development Kits (Software Development Kits, SDKs) are used, limited device and ad-related data may be processed by third-party providers for ad delivery and measurement
  • Medication plans and logs are not used for advertising or sent by us for ad targeting
  • Ad personalization can be adjusted in device settings and Google ad settings

See the privacy policy for details.

🛡️ Notifications & permissions (Android)

CheckMED may use the following for accurate reminders.

Exact alarms

  • Purpose: fire at the exact medication times you configure.
  • Scope: used only for medication alarms — not for ads or analytics.
  • Settings: some devices require an “exact alarm” permission grant.

Foreground service

  • Purpose: deliver alarms and play sounds even when the app is not in the foreground.
  • Scope: only for medication notifications; no collection or transmission of medication data for this purpose.

Lock screen & full-screen alerts

  • Behavior: depending on settings, alerts may appear on the lock screen or as full-screen notifications.
  • Control: you can change presentation in system notification settings anytime.
  • Purpose: help you notice dose times.
