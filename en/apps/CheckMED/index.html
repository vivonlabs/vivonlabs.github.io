📱 CheckMED
Medication reminders · alarms · schedules · intake log

CheckMED is a smart medication management app that keeps reminders, schedules, and intake logs simple.
It helps you avoid missing doses and review recurring plans and progress at a glance.
Personal information is stored on your device (local storage), not on our servers, for safer everyday use.

※ CheckMED is an aid for medication management. It is not a medical device or medical service.

  • 🕒 Caregivers who want simple reminders for parents who often forget doses
  • 👵 Anyone who needs an easy reminder app for less tech‑savvy family members
  • 💊 People managing long‑term medication schedules
  • 📅 Anyone who wants alarms and intake logs in one lightweight place
  • 🔐 Users who care about privacy for health‑related data
  • ⚡ Anyone who prefers a fast, simple app over heavy “health suite” tools

Whether you support a family member’s schedule or need reminders yourself,
CheckMED can be a straightforward solution.

💊 Focused on reminders

CheckMED focuses on reminders, alarms, and intake logs.

  • Enter clinic/pharmacy names, treatment period, and time windows → full schedule generated
  • Recurring plans managed automatically
  • Mark taken / missed and keep a history
  • Per‑plan progress (completion rate, remaining doses)
  • Long‑term schedule management

If you are looking for a medication alarm, schedule helper, or intake log, try CheckMED.

🔔 Reliable notification design

CheckMED is designed with notification reliability in mind.

  • Alarms aligned to the times you set
  • Notifications at the scheduled time regardless of whether the app is open
  • Time‑based scheduling to reduce delays and missed alerts
  • Simple create/edit flows and a clear UI

※ Actual behavior may vary due to device settings, battery optimization, and manufacturer policies.

🧭 Quick start

  1. Create a plan: tap “+” → enter clinic/pharmacy/period → set times → save
  2. Get reminders: receive alarms at the configured times
  3. Confirm intake: log as taken or missed
    • Within 30 minutes: waiting (green)
    • Within 1 hour: waiting (orange)
    • After 1 hour: elapsed → marked as missed
  4. Track progress: completion rate / remaining doses in the plan list
  5. Check volume: confirm media volume beforehand

⚠️ Notes

  • Long plans (stable reminders): for stable behavior, we recommend opening the app periodically to keep state fresh.
  • Long plans (processing time): editing plans, resetting logs, or deleting a plan may take longer when there is a large amount of data.
  • On some devices, battery optimization may change how notifications behave.

🔐 Privacy‑first design

Many medication apps rely on server storage. CheckMED defaults to keeping your data on the device.

  • Plan data in local storage — not uploaded to our servers
  • Sensitive data encrypted with AES‑256; keys protected via Android Keystore / iOS Keychain
  • No account required
  • Requests only minimal permissions
  • When ad SDKs are used, data processing is minimized
  • Ad personalization can be changed anytime in device and Google ad settings

See the privacy policy for details.

🛡️ Notifications & permissions (Android)

CheckMED may use the following for accurate reminders.

Exact alarms

  • Purpose: fire at the exact medication times you configure.
  • Scope: used only for medication alarms — not for ads or analytics.
  • Settings: some devices require an “exact alarm” permission grant.

Foreground service

  • Purpose: deliver alarms and play sounds even when the app is not in the foreground.
  • Scope: only for medication notifications; no collection or transmission of personal data for this purpose.

Lock screen & full‑screen alerts

  • Behavior: depending on settings, alerts may appear on the lock screen or as full‑screen notifications.
  • Control: you can change presentation in system notification settings anytime.
  • Purpose: help you notice dose times reliably.
