Privacy Policy

CheckMed (“the app”) respects your privacy.
This policy explains what information is processed and how while you use the app.

The app is local‑first: no accounts, no server storage of your medication data, and no upload of that data by us.

1. Categories of information

The following may be stored only on your device:

Information you enter

  • Clinic name
  • Pharmacy name
  • Medication name (optional)
  • Condition name (optional)
  • Start date and treatment period
  • Dose times and notification settings
  • Taken / missed logs

Information generated automatically

  • Schedule data derived from your plans
  • Notification scheduling metadata
  • Timestamps when you confirm intake (for logging)

⚠️ The app does not collect your name, phone number, email, precise location, or account credentials for app use.

2. Purposes of use

On‑device data is used only to:

  • Create and manage medication schedules
  • Deliver dose reminders
  • Show taken/missed history
  • Show in‑app statistics and progress

3. Storage and security

  • Data stays in the app’s sandbox on your device.
  • We do not operate a backend that stores your medication records.
  • Stored data is protected with AES‑256 encryption.
  • Keys are protected using Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
  • Schedule and log data may be health‑related; it is processed on device and not transmitted by us to external servers for storage.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or provide your medication records (clinic/pharmacy/medication names/logs) to third parties.

  • Analytics SDKs such as Google Analytics for medication data ❌
  • Uploading medication data to our servers ❌

4‑1. Advertising (AdMob)

The app may use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support operations.

Purposes:

  • Deliver and display ads
  • Maintain service stability
  • Fraud prevention and ad quality

Information that may be processed by Google/partners:

  • Advertising ID (or similar app/instance identifiers)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size, language, etc.)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

⚠️ Medication plans and logs inside the app are not used for advertising or sent for ad targeting.

Related policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data remains on your device while you use the app.
  • You may delete it at any time using the methods below.

How to delete

  • Uninstall the app: data is removed from the device.
  • Use the in‑app “reset data” feature: data is removed according to that feature’s behavior.

Because we do not store your medication records on our servers, there is no separate “cloud copy” to erase on our side.

6. Permissions

The app may request permissions needed to provide reminders:

Notifications

  • Purpose: deliver dose reminders

Exact alarms (where applicable)

  • Purpose: fire alarms at the times you configure
  • Note: helps reduce delays under doze/background restrictions on some devices

Vibration / sound

  • Purpose: make reminders easier to notice

Battery optimization exceptions (optional)

  • Purpose: reduce cases where reminders are blocked by power saving
  • You may refuse; the app may still be usable with reduced notification reliability

Foreground service (where applicable)

  • Purpose: improve reliability and audio playback for notifications when needed
  • Scope: medication notifications only; not used to collect or transmit personal data

Lock screen / full‑screen alerts

  • Behavior: depending on settings, alerts may appear on the lock screen or as full‑screen notifications
  • Purpose: help you notice dose times
  • Control: you can change presentation in system notification settings

The app does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or broad file access for core functionality.

7. Medical information and limitations

The app is not a medical device and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

  • Schedules are based on information you enter.
  • We do not provide medical judgment or treatment.
  • The app assists with logging and reminders only.
  • If you suspect an emergency or adverse reaction, do not rely on the app—contact a qualified provider or emergency services for your region (for example 911, 112, 119, or your local number).
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of data you enter; we are not liable for outcomes caused by errors or omissions.
  • Notifications may be delayed or unavailable due to OS policies, permissions, focus modes, battery optimization, reboots, time zone changes, and other factors outside our control.
  • Disclaimer: to the fullest extent permitted by law, we disclaim liability for damages arising from use or inability to use the app, including delayed/missed notifications, data loss, or input errors.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 to collect personal information, and does not target children with separate experiences requiring collection.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change. Material changes may be communicated via in‑app notices or store listings.
