📱 Houriva
Gentle nudges · On-time alerts · Routines · Focus · Mind care

Houriva is a gentle reminder app that delivers short, soft nudges right on schedule.
It is designed to help you keep small, easy-to-miss routines—like health, focus, and emotional self-care—going in a lighter, calmer way.
Instead of heavy to-do systems or rigid habit tracking, it focuses on a single notification that arrives in the moment you need it.
Your core settings are stored on your device (locally) by default, and the app may include Google AdMob banner ads.

Houriva is a companion app for everyday routines and self-care. It is not a medical device, a medical service, or an emergency service.

Houriva is designed so recurring reminders are delivered as reliably as possible.

  • Schedules repeating alerts based on the weekdays, hours, and minutes you set
  • Structured so alerts can fire even when the app is not open—within device and OS limits
  • On Android, designed with exact alarms and post-reboot recovery in mind

  • Simple setup and a clear UI so you can get going quickly

Actual behavior may vary with device settings, notification permissions, battery optimization, Focus modes, manufacturer policies, reboots, time or time-zone changes, and other OS conditions.

🧭 Quick start guide

You can get started quickly without a maze of settings.

  1. Pick a style: Default, Health, Focus, Mind, or Custom
  2. Set your schedule: Choose weekdays, hour, and minute
  3. Turn alerts on: Start from the home screen
  4. Fine-tune: Adjust sound, vibration, and voice (TTS) if you like
  5. Keep the routine: Receive gentle nudges through the day

⚠️ Good to know

  • On some Android devices, you may need to allow the “exact alarm” permission for time-accurate alerts.
  • On some devices, behavior may differ because of battery optimization or manufacturer policies.
  • After a reboot or a time / time-zone change, we recommend checking that alerts are still on.
  • Alert sounds and voice prompts follow your device volume and system settings.

🛡️ Notifications and permissions (Android)

For stable recurring alerts, Houriva may use the following permissions or system features.

Exact alarms

  • Purpose: Deliver alerts closer to the times you set
  • Scope: Used only for recurring reminders
  • Settings: Some devices require you to allow “exact alarm” access

Notification permission

  • Purpose: Show notifications on your device
  • Scope: Deliver the routine alerts you configure
  • Control: You can change this anytime in system settings

Battery optimization exception

  • Purpose: Reduce the chance of missed alerts on some devices
  • Scope: Optional guidance to improve notification reliability
  • Control: You choose whether to allow or change it

Vibration and boot recovery

  • Purpose: Vibration alerts and restoring scheduled alerts after a reboot
  • Scope: Used only to provide the alert features you set up
